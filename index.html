HootHint

Pick a region of your screen, and HootHint watches it and uses Google's Gemini AI to help with whatever's showing — answered in a small on-screen popup or sent straight to your phone via Telegram.

⬇ Download for Windows Getting Started guide

Free installer · Windows 10/11 · uses your own free Gemini API key

🖱️

Drag to select a region

Choose any part of any monitor. HootHint watches just that region, on a timer you control.

🧠

Your own Gemini key

Free at aistudio.google.com. HootHint never sends your screenshots anywhere but Google's API and, optionally, Telegram.

🔔

Popup or Telegram

See the answer in a small always-on-top popup, or get it pushed straight to your phone.

🦉

Runs quietly in the tray

Minimizes to the system tray once started — check status or stop it any time.

New here? The Getting Started guide walks through installing, getting a free Gemini key, and running your first capture — takes about five minutes.
